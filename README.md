# StreakSafe - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

StreakSafe helps you track small habits and commit to change through pledging optional donation pledges. 

<h2>Information That Will Be Stored In Application</h2>
habit names, check-ins, streak counts, pledge amounts, charity choices, reminder time, and screen-time numbers you enter. In the current build this stays in local storage on your device.</p>
<h2>Notifications</h2>
<p>If you allow notifications, we use them only to remind you to check in at the time you chose.</p>
<h2>Screen time</h2>
<p>If you grant Screen Time or usage access in a future version, we use it to measure counted app time for the Reduce screen time habit. You can exclude apps. We do not sell this data.</p>
<h2>Payments and donations</h2>
<p>Version 1 records pledges on device. When live payments launch, card data will be handled by a payment processor, not stored in full on our servers.</p>
