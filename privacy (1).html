StreakSafe Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

StreakSafe helps you track small habits and optional donation pledges. This policy explains what the app stores.

Data we store on your device

Habit names, check-ins, streak counts, pledge amounts, charity choices, reminder time, and screen-time numbers you enter. In the current build this stays in local storage on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we use them only to remind you to check in at the time you chose.

Screen time

If you grant Screen Time or usage access in a future version, we use it to measure counted app time for the Reduce screen time habit. You can exclude apps. We do not sell this data.

Payments and donations

Version 1 records pledges on device. When live payments launch, card data will be handled by a payment processor, not stored in full on our servers.
